Learn More About Our Job Portal

Welcome to the Job Application and Recruitment Portal for Pio Duran — a community-focused platform designed to make finding jobs and hiring employees easier, faster, and more accessible.

Our goal is simple: help job seekers discover opportunities and help local employers find the right people for their jobs.

Job Seekers

Create a professional profile, discover available job opportunities, submit applications online, and track your application progress.

Employers

Create company profiles, publish job vacancies, review applicants, shortlist qualified candidates, and manage recruitment information.

PESO Staff

Manage employment information, monitor vacancies, assist applicants and employers, and generate useful employment-related reports.

What Is This System?

The Job Application and Recruitment Portal for Pio Duran is a web-based platform created to support the local employment community of Pio Duran, Albay.

The system allows job seekers to create profiles, search for available jobs, and submit applications online. Employers can create company profiles, post job vacancies, and manage applicants.

PESO staff can monitor job opportunities, manage applicant information, assist job seekers, and generate useful employment reports.

Instead of relying only on social media posts, printed announcements, word-of-mouth, or manual applications, this portal provides one convenient place where job opportunities and applicants can meet.

Why Was This System Created?

Finding a job should not be difficult simply because information is scattered.

Job opportunities may be posted in different Facebook pages, groups, bulletin boards, or through personal contacts. At the same time, employers may have difficulty reaching qualified applicants, while PESO staff may spend additional time managing applicant information manually.

This system aims to help solve these challenges by providing a centralized and organized online employment platform.

Job Seekers

  • Add your education and qualifications
  • Add skills and work experience
  • Search available job vacancies
  • View job details and requirements
  • Submit applications online
  • Track your application status
  • Receive important application notifications

Employers

  • Create an employer or company profile
  • Post job vacancies
  • Specify qualifications and requirements
  • Review submitted applications
  • View applicant profiles
  • Shortlist qualified applicants
  • Update application statuses
  • Manage recruitment information

PESO Staff

  • Manage job seeker information

  • Assist applicants
  • Monitor recruitment activities
  • Manage employer information
  • Generate employment-related reports
  • Track hiring outcomes

For Job Seekers

It provides easier access to job opportunities without having to visit different places or search through numerous social media posts.

For Employers

It provides a more organized way to advertise vacancies and find potential employees.

For PESO

It can help organize employment information and support faster employment facilitation.

For the Community

The portal can encourage local hiring, reduce barriers to employment, and connect people with opportunities closer to home.

What Makes Our Portal Different?

Unlike large international career platforms, this system focuses specifically on the Pio Duran community.

Our vision is to create a platform where:

Local opportunities can reach local people, and local talent can help build the local community.

Local Opportunities

Help residents discover employment opportunities available within and around the local community.

Career Resources

Provide a centralized platform where applicants can organize their professional information and explore career opportunities.

Community Employment

Strengthen connections between local businesses, job seekers, and employment assistance services.

Safety and Trust

Because job applications involve personal information, protecting user data should be an important part of the system.

Account Security

  • Secure account authentication
  • Responsible handling of personal information
  • Privacy-conscious applicant profiles

Employer Trust

  • Verified employer accounts where applicable
  • Clear reporting mechanisms
  • Reporting of suspicious job postings

Data Protection

  • Secure information storage
  • Regular data backups
  • Responsible information management
