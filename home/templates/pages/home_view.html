Learn More About Our Job Portal

Welcome to the Job Application and Recruitment Portal for Pio Duran — a community-focused platform designed to make finding jobs and hiring employees easier, faster, and more accessible.

Our goal is simple: help job seekers discover opportunities and help local employers find the right people for their jobs.

Job Seekers

Create a professional profile, discover available opportunities, submit applications, and monitor application progress.

Employers

Create company profiles, publish vacancies, review applicants, and manage recruitment information.

PESO Staff

Manage employment information, monitor vacancies, assist users, and generate useful employment reports.

What Is This System?

The Job Application and Recruitment Portal for Pio Duran is a web-based platform created to support the local employment community of Pio Duran, Albay.

The system allows job seekers to create profiles, search for available jobs, and submit applications online. Employers can create company profiles, post job vacancies, and manage applicants.

PESO staff can monitor job opportunities, manage applicant information, assist job seekers, and generate useful employment reports.

Why Was This System Created?

Finding a job should not be difficult simply because information is scattered.

Job opportunities may be posted across different social media pages, groups, bulletin boards, or personal contacts. At the same time, employers may have difficulty reaching qualified applicants.

This system provides a centralized and organized platform where job opportunities and applicants can connect.

Make job opportunities easier to find
Make applications more convenient
Help employers reach potential applicants
Organize applicant information
Reduce unnecessary paperwork
Improve applicant-employer communication

  • Track application status

Employers

  • Post job vacancies
  • Specify qualifications
  • Review applications
  • View applicant profiles
  • Shortlist applicants
  • Manage recruitment information

PESO Staff

  • Manage job seeker information
  • Monitor vacancies
  • Assist applicants
  • Monitor recruitment activities
  • Manage employer information
  • Generate reports

How Does It Help the Community?

The portal is designed to become a digital bridge between local talent and local employment opportunities.

For Job Seekers

Easier access to job opportunities without searching through many different sources.

For Employers

A more organized way to advertise vacancies and connect with potential employees.

For PESO

Better organization of employment information and employment assistance activities.

What Makes Our Portal Different?

Unlike large international career platforms, this system focuses specifically on the Pio Duran community.

The vision is to create a platform where local opportunities can reach local people, while local talent can help build the local community.

Local Opportunities

Help residents discover employment opportunities available within and around the community.

Career Resources

Give applicants a centralized place to manage their professional information.

Community Employment

Strengthen connections between businesses, job seekers, and employment services.
